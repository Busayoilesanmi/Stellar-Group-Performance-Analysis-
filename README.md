📌 Project Overview
The project aimed to uncover revenue drivers, customer behavior, regional trends, and sales representative performance to guide data-driven business decisions.

The Goal of this analysis is to evaluate sales performance to support business decisions on customer behavior, top products, regions, and team performance. By identifying revenue trends and high-value customers, this report offers data-driven recommendations to enhance profitability and efficiency.
